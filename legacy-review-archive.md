# Legacy Review Archive

> **REVIEW ARCHIVE — Juris Studies is the main version.** These historical readings are retained for comparison and editorial review. They may contain outdated text, broken references, unresolved merge conflicts, or unfinished work.

Use the main [Juris Studies Sourcebooks and Primers](https://profmackelly.gitbook.io/learn/) for the current teaching version.

### Legacy book snapshots

The downloadable archives below preserve 348 divergent or unmatched readings from six legacy books as of November 2, 2026. Each package includes the original Markdown snapshot, a source revision manifest, candidate links to the main version, and a review copy with legacy-relative links made absolute. Existing legacy sites remain available.

* Criminal Law Sourcebook — 5 readings
* Introduction to Law and the Courts — 31 readings
* Bioethics and the Law — 72 readings
* Business Law I — 89 readings
* Criminal Justice Liability Law — 39 readings
* Criminal Procedure — 112 readings

Identical exports and text-equivalent copies remain recorded in the comparison report. Private drafts and unrelated working spaces remain separate. Images and other media in the Markdown continue to use the existing public source URLs.

Pre-existing broken references and conflict markers are retained and flagged for review; archiving does not approve their accuracy.
